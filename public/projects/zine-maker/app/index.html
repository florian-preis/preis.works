Format

Paper

One photo on the back of the sheet, revealed when unfolded. Requires printing on both sides, and the cut runs through the middle of the poster.

The inside of the front and back cover stays blank, so the cover sheet can go on card.

mm

10 mm is the margin of the printer I use. Set it to what your printer model needs: every page gets a white frame of this width.

Printer calibration

Some printers shrink every page a little, so the folds miss the gaps between pages. Print the test sheet at actual size, measure lines A and B, and enter the lengths.

Page numbers

Bottom of each photo page, in the caption typeface. Page 1 is the first page with a photo.
