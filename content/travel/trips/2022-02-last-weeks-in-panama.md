title: Last weeks in Panama
start: 2022-02
countries: [PA]
with: Ellen
---

We spent our final weeks in Panama exploring the San Blas Islands with their white sand and turquoise water. One night on a remote island, someone repeatedly knocked on our door and tried to get in. Ellen was scared, so I found an old knife and a wooden stick and stood guard outside while she tried to sleep. We also finally did the helicopter tour over Panama City that I had gifted her for our first anniversary apart. At the end of the month, Ellen left for New York and I packed up my life in Panama to return to Denmark after six amazing months.
