title: Easter on Bornholm
start: 2021-03
countries: [DK]

---

After a COVID-ridden autumn and winter, we spent Easter in a summer house on Bornholm. We took the train with our bikes and cycled onto the ferry. The house was close to the beach and we went for sunset walks almost every evening. For a few days, we rented a car to explore more of the island, but most of the time we cooked, read, and enjoyed the quiet.
