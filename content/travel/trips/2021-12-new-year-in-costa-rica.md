Between Christmas and New Year, we travelled to Costa Rica and stayed near Manuel Antonio National Park. Our New Year's dinner on the beach was interrupted by heavy rain and there were almost no fireworks because we were inside a national park. It was still a special way to start the year. We continued towards La Fortuna where the volcano remained hidden behind clouds, but we found an amazing local barbecue place we returned to several times. When Nicaragua closed its borders due to COVID, we quickly changed plans and booked flights to Bogotá instead.
