Geography quizzes have been part of my life for as long as I can remember, from guessing number plates on family holidays with my brother and dad to trivia apps on my phone. Building my own meant I could keep just the game modes I enjoy most.

Less about filling a gap, more about reconnecting with an old favourite I had got a bit rusty at.
