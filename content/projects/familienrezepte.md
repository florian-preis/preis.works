Lena's book is a single copy, and the recipes in it belong to the whole family. I wanted everyone to be able to open it, wherever they happen to be cooking, without anyone having to borrow my copy.

What mattered most was keeping the look and feel Lena gave it: her handwriting, her watercolours, and the quiet pleasure of turning a page. So the book on the screen is a black notebook that opens like the real thing, and the recipes inside are hers, down to the dialect words and the dishes that never had exact amounts. Now the whole family can cook from it, and it is still the book she made for me.
